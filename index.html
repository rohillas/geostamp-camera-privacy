Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Geo Stamping is a camera app that stamps each photo with the time, GPS coordinates, and address where it was taken. This page explains exactly what the app accesses, what it does with it, and what never leaves your phone.

01Information we collect

Geo Stamping collects only what it needs to stamp your photos:

  • Photos you take through the app's camera screen.
  • Precise location (GPS) at the moment you shoot, used to print coordinates on the photo and center the map thumbnail.
  • Reverse-geocoded address — a human-readable street address resolved from those coordinates.

The app does not collect your name, contacts, browsing activity, or any identifier tied to you as a person. There is no account or sign-in.

02Permissions at a glance

CAMERALets you take the photo. No image leaves your device except the one you choose to save or share.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATIONPrecise GPS fix, used to stamp exact coordinates and center the map thumbnail.
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATIONFallback location source on devices/situations where a precise fix isn't available.
INTERNETNeeded only to fetch the small map thumbnail image and to resolve the street address — see Section 4.

03How it's used

Location and address data are used for exactly one purpose: printing them, along with the timestamp, onto the photo you take, and writing the GPS coordinates into that photo's EXIF metadata so your gallery app can also show where it was taken. None of this is used for advertising, profiling, or analytics — Geo Stamping contains no ad SDK and no analytics SDK.

04Third-party services

Two requests leave your device, both scoped to the single photo you're about to take:

  • Google Static Maps API — receives only the latitude and longitude of your current position, to render the small map thumbnail shown in the app and stamped onto the photo. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Android's on-device Geocoder — resolves coordinates into a street address using your phone's own geocoding service (backed by Google Play services on most devices), governed by the same Google privacy policy above.

Neither call includes your name, device identifiers beyond what the Android OS attaches to any network request, or any other app data.

05Storage & retention

Stamped photos are saved directly to your device's photo library (MediaStore) — the same place every photo from your regular camera app goes. Geo Stamping has no backend server and no cloud storage of its own; nothing about your photos, locations, or addresses is retained by us anywhere, at any time.

06Data sharing & sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not share it with anyone except the two Google services described in Section 4, which exist to render the map thumbnail and address text inside your own photo.

07Children's privacy

Geo Stamping is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

08Your choices

  • Revoke camera or location access anytime in Settings → Apps → Geo Stamping → Permissions.
  • Delete any stamped photo the same way you'd delete any other photo in your gallery.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored locally — there's no server-side account to separately delete.

09Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
